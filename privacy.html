Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Summary: ClipCycle is designed as a local-first creator tool. The current desktop workflow keeps imported post data and downloaded media on the user’s own computer.

This Privacy Policy explains how ClipCycle (“ClipCycle”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal data when you visit this website, contact us, use the ClipCycle desktop application or connect supported third-party services.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

ClipCycle is an independent creator-tool project based in Ireland and acts as the controller for personal data it receives directly. Contact: clipcycle.creator@gmail.com.

2. Information we may process

Website and contact information

  • Your email address and information included when you contact us.
  • Basic hosting and security logs that may be processed by the website host.

Desktop application information

  • Post metadata you choose to import, such as dates, titles, locations, sounds, likes and media links.
  • Video, photo and cover-image files you choose to archive.
  • Creator preferences, including cooldowns, priority labels, exclusions, captions and posting plans.
  • Local history, including selected, skipped, uploaded and posted status.

TikTok connection information

When you choose to connect a TikTok account, TikTok handles the login screen. ClipCycle may receive account identifiers, authorization scopes and access credentials needed to provide the feature you requested. ClipCycle does not ask for or receive your TikTok password.

3. How we use information

  • To import and organise the archive you request.
  • To select eligible older posts according to your settings.
  • To generate and save post-planning information.
  • To send content to TikTok when you expressly request it.
  • To answer support, privacy and security enquiries.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and protect the service.

4. Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performing a contract or taking steps at your request, consent, legal obligations and legitimate interests in operating and securing ClipCycle without overriding your rights.

5. Local storage and transfers

The current desktop version stores imported data, downloaded media, settings and history locally on the computer where it runs. This information is not automatically uploaded to ClipCycle. When you request a TikTok upload, the selected media and required authorization information are transmitted to TikTok to perform that request.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared only as needed:

  • With TikTok when you connect your account or request a content upload.
  • With providers that host the public website or support essential security and delivery functions.
  • When required by law or needed to protect users, ClipCycle or others from fraud, abuse or security threats.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Local archive data remains on your device until you delete it.
  • You can remove local files, the ClipCycle database and prepared-post folders from your computer.
  • You may revoke third-party authorization through the relevant account settings.
  • Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary.

8. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or receive a copy of personal data, object to certain processing and withdraw consent. You may complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission or your local supervisory authority.

To exercise a right, email clipcycle.creator@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity.

9. Cookies and analytics

ClipCycle does not currently place advertising cookies or operate its own behavioural analytics on this website. The website host may process standard technical request information under its own terms and privacy notices.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the service. No system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should protect their computer, TikTok account, developer credentials, tokens and exported data files.

11. Children

ClipCycle is not directed to children under 13. Users must also satisfy TikTok’s age requirements and applicable law.

12. Third-party services

TikTok and the website host operate under their own privacy policies. ClipCycle is not responsible for their independent privacy practices.

13. Changes

We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the current version.

14. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to clipcycle.creator@gmail.com.
