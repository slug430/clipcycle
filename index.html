How ClipCycle works

A clear workflow designed around the creator’s approval.

Sign in

Use a one-time link sent to your email. There is no password to remember or lose.

Connect

Link your TikTok account and we read your posts and their view counts. Suggestions are ready in seconds, with nothing to download.

Decide

Get one suggestion a day with the reasoning behind it. Approve it or skip to the next.

Send or post

Approved clips land in your TikTok drafts for you to finish and publish. On Premium you can instead review the post settings in ClipCycle and publish it to TikTok yourself, without leaving the app.

Common questions

Is ClipCycle free?

There is a free plan that imports your posts, archives up to 100 of them and gives you the priority labels, cooldowns and daily prompt. Pro adds unlimited archiving, AI-written hooks and captions, archive insights, TikTok draft delivery and creator-confirmed posting straight to TikTok for €8 a month. Your first five Pro posts are free and need no card. See the Pricing page.

Does ClipCycle publish videos automatically?

No. Nothing is ever posted on a schedule, in the background, or without you. On the Free plan an approved video is sent to your TikTok drafts, and you make the final publishing decision inside TikTok. On Premium you can publish from ClipCycle instead — but only after you grant TikTok’s posting permission, and only once you have confirmed that specific post: its privacy setting, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, and any commercial-content disclosure. You can leave the feature switched off and keep using drafts.

Does ClipCycle guarantee more views or followers?

No. It is an organisation and workflow tool. Reach and performance depend on content, audience response and platform systems.

Who owns the uploaded videos?

The creator retains ownership. Users must only process or upload content they own or are authorised to use.

Where is archive data stored?

On ClipCycle’s servers in the European Union. Media is held in private storage that is never publicly listable, and previews are served through short-lived signed links. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

Can I get my data back out?

Yes. You can export a copy of your data from inside the app, and deleting your account removes your stored media as well as your records.

Put ClipCycle on your home screen

There is nothing to download. Your phone makes the app itself, from the page — it takes two taps and about a second.

iPhone & iPad Safari

  1. Open app.clipcycle.co in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add

It has to be Safari. Chrome on iPhone cannot add to the home screen.

Android Chrome

  1. Open app.clipcycle.co in Chrome
  2. Tap the three-dot menu, top right
  3. Tap Install app, or Add to Home screen
  4. Tap Install

Chrome often offers this by itself. If a bar appears at the bottom asking to install, that is the same thing — just tap it.

You are on a computer. It installs here too — look for the install icon in your address bar — but ClipCycle is built for a phone, so it is worth opening this page there.
