Common questions

Does ClipCycle publish videos automatically?

No. The intended draft-upload workflow sends a selected video to TikTok so the creator can open it, edit it and make the final publishing decision.

Does ClipCycle guarantee more views or followers?

No. It is an organisation and workflow tool. Reach and performance depend on content, audience response and platform systems.

Who owns the uploaded videos?

The creator retains ownership. Users must only process or upload content they own or are authorised to use.

Where is archive data stored?

The current desktop workflow stores imported post information and downloaded media locally on the user’s computer.
