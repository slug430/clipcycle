A few things to know

Curious? Good.

Do I need an audience already?

No. Beginners can start with their goals, interests and available equipment. Established creators can bring their post history and recorded results into the process.

Which platforms can I use?

TikTok connects today. You can also plan and prepare content for Instagram and YouTube Shorts, then export, post and record results manually. Direct connections for those platforms are coming soon.

Will you post without asking me?

No. You choose what to send to TikTok drafts, and direct posting requires your review and confirmation for each post. A plan is not an unattended publishing schedule.

Can it import all my TikTok videos automatically?

Connecting TikTok can bring in supported account and post information. Media availability depends on the available import route; you can also upload your own footage. A separate automatic video import connection is in preparation and is not live yet.

Can I leave or get my content back?

Yes. Manage your subscription, download account data and request account deletion in Settings. Download video files separately from Library. See the Privacy Policy for details.
