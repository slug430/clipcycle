Product overview

A practical archive-to-publishing workflow.

ClipCycle combines secure archiving, post selection, reuse safeguards and creator-confirmed TikTok delivery in one web app you can use from your phone or laptop.

↓

Export importer

Reads your posts and their view counts from your connected TikTok account, and the data export you upload for the media itself, organising both into one library.

▣

Secure media archive

Copies your own media into private EU storage before TikTok’s temporary export links expire, which happens within days.

★

Priority controls

Mark posts as high priority, seasonal, paused or never to be reused.

↻

Reuse cooldowns

Set a minimum number of days before an older post becomes eligible again.

✎

Post planning

Prepare revised hooks, captions, hashtags and a recommended posting time.

➜

TikTok draft delivery

Where authorised, send a selected video into TikTok’s inbox flow for final editing and publication by the creator.

◉

Post from ClipCycle

On Premium, review the whole post in ClipCycle — privacy, comments, Duet, Stitch, cover frame and any commercial-content disclosure — then publish it to TikTok yourself. It needs a separate TikTok permission you grant once, and each post still takes your confirmation.

◔

Daily prompt

One notification a day at the hour you choose, in your own time zone, with email as a fallback.

◈

Archive insights

Patterns drawn from your own history — the days, times and themes your posts have actually done best on.

⇩

Export and erasure

Download a copy of everything, or delete your account and its media, without emailing anyone.

Selection modes with different goals

Creators choose how ClipCycle prioritises eligible archive posts.

  • Second chance: favors older clips with limited reach.
  • Balanced: combines age, history and engagement.
  • Proven winner: favors stronger previous performance.
  • Random: respects exclusions and cooldowns.

Designed around informed user action.

TikTok authentication uses TikTok’s own authorization flow. ClipCycle never asks for your TikTok password, and never posts on its own initiative — posting to TikTok requires a permission you grant and a confirmation you give for that individual post.
