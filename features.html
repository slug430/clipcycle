01 / coach

A coach that starts with you.

  • Tailored onboarding covers your experience, niche, subjects, equipment, preferred formats, goals and available time.
  • Video ideas and practical prompts help you get from “I should post” to a specific next step.
  • Your voice, saved context, available footage and recorded results inform relevant suggestions.
  • Suggestions are guidance to review and adapt. More context helps; it does not guarantee performance.
02 / ideas

Catch it before it wanders off.

  • Save an idea as a quick note or use the microphone to capture it by voice where supported.
  • Browse compact idea cards, search your collection and open a title for the full brief.
  • Develop a thought into a video direction, then add it to a plan or take it into Create.
03 / editor

Make your footage feel like you.

  • Trim and split clips, arrange a sequence and adjust playback speed.
  • Change framing, colour and sound; add hooks, text and captions.
  • Reuse saved styles for a consistent look. Review detected old text and choose supported cover options.
  • Preview your edits and export a new version. Keep your original footage. Automated checks can miss details, so review the final clip.
04 / reposts

There’s still life in that library.

  • Organise uploaded footage and supported TikTok post information in one searchable place.
  • Explore suggestions using performance, age and reuse history. Prioritise, pause or skip clips.
  • Prepare a fresh hook, caption and hashtags in your style.
  • On eligible plans, send a selected clip to TikTok drafts or review and confirm a direct post. Nothing publishes unattended.
05 / plans

A rhythm you can actually keep.

  • Choose a 4, 8 or 12-week direction and work through manageable weekly plans.
  • Bring together fresh video ideas, saved briefs and clips worth revisiting.
  • Track the work from planned to created and posted. Adjust the plan when your week changes.
  • Use post results to choose what to repeat, improve or experiment with next.
06 / results

Turn numbers into a next step.

  • See your Dashboard for an overview and review individual post results.
  • Compare recorded performance and investigate clips that underperformed.
  • Use supported TikTok statistics and manually recorded Instagram or YouTube Shorts results.
  • Keep platform results distinguishable. Numbers can be incomplete or delayed, and comparisons are clues rather than proof of cause.
