Questions about paying

Who am I actually buying from?

Polar acts as the merchant of record for ClipCycle. Polar is the seller on your receipt, takes the payment and handles any VAT or sales tax due in your country. ClipCycle never sees or stores your card details.

What happens when the trial ends?

You are told before it ends. If you do nothing, the subscription starts. If you cancel before it ends, you are not charged and your account moves to the Free plan with your archive intact.

Can I get a refund?

Email us within 14 days of a charge and we will refund it, no argument, as long as the plan has not been heavily used in that period. Where consumer law gives you a statutory right of withdrawal, that right applies on top of this and is not reduced by it.

What happens to my media if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted without you asking. If you drop below the Free plan's archive limit you keep read access and can export everything; you just cannot add new posts until you are back under the limit or upgrade again.

Do you offer anything for students or early users?

Yes — email clipcycle.creator@gmail.com and tell us what you are working on.
